# Substrate

## Overview
- **Type:** Tech startup → media/streaming conglomerate
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco
- **Origin:** Failing game engine company (competing against Unity, Unreal, Godot — losing)

## The Pivot
Discovered [Elia](../characters/elia.md) had built [Rayan](../science/rayan.md) on their hardware
after hours. Legally, they owned it. Pivoted from game engine to streaming platform built around
Rayan. Full story: [The Discovery](../history/the-discovery.md)

## The Streaming Service
Two ways to experience Rayan:
- **Produced episodes** — edited, narrative-driven, scheduled. The majority watches these.
- **Live streams** — raw, unedited feeds. A smaller community watches these and knows the difference.

For the full dynamic: [The Edit](../themes/the-edit.md)

## Political Influence
- Sees everything in Rayan, chooses what to show
- Produced episodes shape how audiences think about power, class, conflict, morality — through story,
  not propaganda. Through who gets to be the hero.
- Tracks audience viewing habits — the watchers are also being watched

## Raya's Status
[Raya](../characters/raya.md) doesn't know she's being watched. Effectively immortal within the
simulation — she cannot die, because the show must go on.
