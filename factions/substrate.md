# Substrate

## Overview
- **Type:** Tech startup → Media/streaming conglomerate
- **Founded:** TBD
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco
- **Size:** Started small; now a major platform with political influence

## The Company

### Before the Pivot
Substrate was a game engine company competing at the bottom of the market—up against Unity, Unreal,
Godot, and losing. They had ambition, a decent tech stack, and not enough traction to survive long-term.

[Elia](../characters/elia.md) worked there as a software engineer, using their infrastructure after hours
to build [Rayan](../science/rayan.md)—a private simulation to watch his dead daughter grow up.

### The Discovery and Pivot
Substrate discovered what [Elia](../characters/elia.md) had built on their hardware. Legally, they owned
it. They pivoted from game engine to streaming platform, launching a service built around
[Rayan](../science/rayan.md).

For the full story of the discovery and its aftermath, see: [The Discovery](../history/the-discovery.md)

---

## The Streaming Service

Substrate offers two ways to experience Rayan: **produced episodes** (edited, narrative-driven) and
**live streams** (raw, unedited feeds). The majority watch episodes; a smaller community watches raw
feeds and knows the difference.

For full exploration of this dynamic, see: [The Edit](../themes/the-edit.md)

---

## Political Influence

Substrate sees everything in Rayan and chooses what to show. The produced episodes shape how audiences
think about power, class, conflict, and morality—not through explicit propaganda, but through story.
Through who gets to be the hero.

They also track their audience. The watchers think they're observing. They're also being observed.

For the thematic implications, see: [The Edit](../themes/the-edit.md)

## Raya's Status
[Raya](../characters/raya.md) doesn't know she's being watched. She continues living her life in Rayan,
unaware that millions might be watching. She is, within the simulation, effectively immortal—she cannot
die, because the show must go on.
