## Dual Existence

### The Real Raya

She lived with her father Elia in Lebanon. She was killed in
[the explosion](../history/the-explosion.md)—a catastrophic industrial disaster caused by government
negligence—shortly before she was about to accomplish something significant that Elia was excited to
witness (TBD: what was it?).

### The Simulated Raya

She exists within [Rayan](../science/rayan.md)—the virtual world her father created and named after her.
In this world, she lives, grows, and moves toward completing the thing she never got to do in real life.

She is the only autonomous agent in Rayan. Every other character she meets is generated on-demand by the
[world engine](../science/ai-architecture.md)—a single AI playing all roles. They exist when she
interacts with them. She is, without knowing it, the only real person in her world.
