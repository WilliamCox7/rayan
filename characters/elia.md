## Background
Lebanese Christian immigrant. Came to America after his young daughter Raya was killed in
[the explosion](../history/the-explosion.md). She died shortly before accomplishing something he was
eagerly anticipating (TBD: what?). He now lives in San Francisco, spending his nights building an
elaborate virtual world.

## Personality
- Driven by grief and inability to let go
- Obsessive, meticulous — years of painstaking detail in his world
- Avoidant of real connection; pours everything into the simulation

## The Simulation
Created [Rayan](../science/rayan.md) to watch Raya grow up and complete what she never got to do.
The planet is named for her. It is his attempt at closure — or a way to avoid ever reaching it.

## The Discovery and Firing
Built Rayan on [Substrate's](../factions/substrate.md) hardware after hours. They discovered it,
pivoted the company around it, launched it as a streaming service. He stayed because he couldn't
abandon Raya. Eventually they fired him. Now he watches her on the stream like everyone else —
creator to employee to audience member. Full account: [The Discovery](../history/the-discovery.md)
