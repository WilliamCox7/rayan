## Background

A Lebanese Christian immigrant from Lebanon. He came to America after his young daughter Raya was killed
in [the explosion](../history/the-explosion.md)—a catastrophic industrial disaster caused by years of
government negligence. She died shortly before she was about to accomplish something he was eagerly
anticipating (TBD: what was it?).

His technical background comes from one of Lebanon's strong universities (AUB, LAU) before emigrating.
After the explosion, he could no longer stay. His daughter wasn't killed by an enemy—she was killed by
her own country's corruption and indifference. The betrayal was total.

Now alone and carrying unprocessed trauma, he lives in San Francisco and spends his nights building an
elaborate virtual world.

## The Simulation

He created [Rayan](../science/rayan.md) to watch Raya grow up unharmed and complete the thing she never
got to do. It is his attempt at closure—or perhaps a way to avoid ever reaching it.

The planet's name—Rayan—is derived from his daughter's name. The world itself is named for her.

## The Discovery and Firing

Elia built Rayan on [Substrate's](../factions/substrate.md) hardware, after hours. When they discovered
it, they pivoted the entire company around his creation—launching it as a streaming service. He stayed
at first, unable to abandon Raya. Eventually, they fired him.

Now he can do nothing but watch Raya on the streaming service like everyone else. He went from creator
to employee to audience member. The distance is unbearable.

For the full account, see: [The Discovery](../history/the-discovery.md)
