## Personality

Sharp from growing up watching instead of talking. Reads a room before she
enters it. Processes fast, speaks slow.

Gritty but not hard — genuine curiosity, not naivety. She doesn't flinch
at ugliness but hasn't stopped being surprised by it. Absorbs everything
but holds ideas at arm's length before committing. Slow to trust, deeply
loyal once she does.

Athletic and fast — not trained to fight, but trained to move. Intelligent
in a way no one has formally recognized. It shows in pattern recognition,
unexpected connections, and questions that make grown men uncomfortable.

**Core tension:** Strategist's mind, believer's heart. The story tests
whether she can keep both.

## Background

Lower class. Mother dead or absent (TBD). Raised by her father in his
trade.

Her father (name TBD) is a **debt collector** — works for someone with
power (TBD) and recovers what's owed. Hated for it. People cross the
street. Children told to stay away from his daughter.

He's not cruel — he's *precise*. Knows exactly how much pressure to apply
and when to stop. Controversial because he's unapologetic: doesn't claim
the work is noble, won't accept it makes him evil. Blames the people with
titles and clean hands who built the system, not the ones doing collection.
Hard to argue with. Harder to unhear.

Maren's been at his side since she could walk the routes. She's sat in
rooms where her father decided whether families kept their homes. From this
she learned:

- How power works — door to door, not from books
- How to read people — desperation has a posture, lying has a rhythm
- How systems create suffering — the debts weren't created by the people
  who owe them
- Composure under hostility — she grew up hated by proximity
