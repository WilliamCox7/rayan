# Rayan

## Nature
This world is entirely virtual—a simulation created by a single human mind over years of obsessive,
grief-driven work.

It was built by [Elia](../characters/elia.md) after he lost [Raya](../characters/raya.md). The world
exists so he can watch her grow up unharmed and complete the thing she never got to do.

The planet is named after his daughter—Rayan, derived from Raya. The name itself is an act of grief.

See: [The Unfinished Complex](../themes/the-unfinished-complex.md)

## Technical Reality
- **Medium:** A digital simulation, built after-hours over years
- **Location of creation:** San Francisco (Elia's work computer)
- **Scope:** TBD—is it a full planet? A single region? Does it expand as Elia builds?

See: [AI Architecture](ai-architecture.md) for how the simulation runs.

## The World Itself
Rayan is a fantasy world with historical inspiration—likely drawn from Elia's Levantine roots and
imagination. It has the texture of a place that has existed for centuries: old cities, layered cultures,
inherited grudges, magic woven into daily life.

This is the main canvas of the story. While [Raya](../characters/raya.md) exists here, she is a minor
character. The focus is on other inhabitants—their relationships, ambitions, struggles, loves.

### Tone
- **Romantic:** Love and desire are central, but complicated
- **Confusing:** Motivations are layered; people don't always know what they want
- **Controversial:** No moralizing; characters make ugly and sympathetic choices
- **Nostalgic:** A world with history and weight; loss lingers

### Themes
- [Social Microeconomics](../themes/social-microeconomics.md) — class, status, and relationships as systems of exchange
- [The Unfinished Complex](../themes/the-unfinished-complex.md) — the meta layer of why this world exists
- [Simulation Theory](../themes/simulation-theory.md) — do characters sense something is wrong?
- [The Mirror](../themes/the-mirror.md) — the audience watches AI's reflection of humanity without knowing it
- [The Edit](../themes/the-edit.md) — produced episodes vs live streams; manufactured truth vs reality

### Inhabitants
[Raya](../characters/raya.md) is the only autonomous consciousness. Everyone else is generated by
multiple AI models—each culture managed by its own "cultural engine." They experience their lives as
real, unaware they're reflections of human patterns.

See: [AI Architecture](ai-architecture.md) for technical details, and [The Mirror](../themes/the-mirror.md)
for thematic implications.

## Rules of the Simulation

### Physics
Standard physics apply—except where magic breaks them.

### Magic
Magic is real—it exists in the code. What characters experience as supernatural power is actually
exploitation of the simulation's underlying architecture. But magic is rare, limited by treaties forged
after [the Shattering](../history/the-shattering.md).

See: [The Magic System](../magic/the-system.md)

## Notes
The nature of Rayan raises questions about everything in it. Every culture, creature, and landscape is
either something Elia deliberately designed or something that emerged from his creation. The magic system
could be rules he wrote to give Raya power she didn't have in real life.
