# Rayan

## Nature
A virtual simulation created by [Elia](../characters/elia.md) after losing
[Raya](../characters/raya.md). Built so he can watch her grow up and complete what she never got to
do. Named after his daughter — the name itself is an act of grief. See:
[The Unfinished Complex](../themes/the-unfinished-complex.md)

## Technical Reality
- **Medium:** Digital simulation, built after-hours over years
- **Created in:** San Francisco (Elia's work computer)
- **Architecture:** [AI Architecture](ai-architecture.md)
- **Physics:** [Planetary Physics](planetary-physics.md)

## The World
Fantasy world with Levantine-rooted historical inspiration. Old cities, layered cultures, inherited
grudges, magic woven into daily life. While Raya exists here, she is a minor character — the focus
is on other inhabitants and their stories.

### Tone
- **Romantic** — love and desire central, but complicated
- **Confusing** — layered motivations; people don't always know what they want
- **Controversial** — no moralizing; ugly and sympathetic choices alike
- **Nostalgic** — history and weight; loss lingers

### Themes
- [Social Microeconomics](../themes/social-microeconomics.md) — class, status, relationships as exchange
- [The Unfinished Complex](../themes/the-unfinished-complex.md) — why this world exists
- [Simulation Theory](../themes/simulation-theory.md) — do characters sense something is wrong?
- [The Mirror](../themes/the-mirror.md) — audience watches AI's reflection of humanity
- [The Edit](../themes/the-edit.md) — produced episodes vs live streams

### Inhabitants
Raya is the only autonomous consciousness. Everyone else is generated by cultural engines — each
culture managed by its own AI model. They experience their lives as real. See:
[AI Architecture](ai-architecture.md)

### Magic
Real — exists in the code. What characters experience as supernatural power is exploitation of the
simulation's architecture. Rare, limited by post-Shattering treaties. See:
[The Magic System](../magic/the-system.md)
