# Planetary Physics

## Overview
Rayan is Pluto-sized (~2,377 km diameter) running Earth-standard physics (9.8 m/s² gravity, 24-hour
day cycle, standard atmosphere). Small geometry, normal physics. A deliberate engineering shortcut by
[Elia](../characters/elia.md) — built after hours on a work computer, so he used stock physics to
avoid custom calculations.

## What Feels Normal
Everything physical: gravity, objects falling/bouncing, day/night cycles, weather, how bodies move
and tire. A character dropped into Rayan would notice nothing wrong with physics.

## What Feels Wrong
The geometry betrays the small scale:
- **Close horizon** — ~2-3 km on flat ground (vs ~5 km on Earth). Curvature visible from elevation.
- **Finite world** — circumnavigation in a single lifetime. No location truly remote. Explorers run
  out of world.
- **Navigation oddities** — ships vanish over horizon fast, constellations barely shift when
  traveling, maps can depict the entire world accurately

## Narrative Implications
- Most inhabitants never question it — all they've known
- Perceptive characters might notice (feeds [simulation-theory](../themes/simulation-theory.md))
- Small scale means cultural engines are always in proximity — constant border friction, may have
  contributed to [the Shattering](../history/the-shattering.md)
- Viewers likely don't notice; but travel times and horizon distances wouldn't add up if analyzed
