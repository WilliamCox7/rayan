# AI Architecture

## Overview

Two phases:

1. **Elia's build** — Two agents: Raya + one NPC engine. A keyhole into
   his daughter's life.
2. **Substrate's expansion** — Multiple cultural engines populate the
   entire planet for streaming.

## Raya's Agent

[Raya](../characters/raya.md) is the only autonomous entity in
[Rayan](rayan.md). Persistent memory, her own goals, acts independently.
The only "real" consciousness in a world of reflections.

## Phase 1: Elia's Build

Two agents on a work computer: Raya's agent (autonomous) and a single NPC
engine playing all other characters. The world extended only as far as
Raya's life required — characters activated when she encountered them.
Everything beyond was potential, not rendered reality.

## Phase 2: Substrate's Expansion

The NPC engine was replaced with multiple **cultural engines** — each
managing a distinct culture/region. Different models create genuine
cultural variation. Characters generate on-demand at cultural boundaries,
producing real friction and collision.

Every model trained on human data. Each culture is a distillation of human
patterns. See: [The Mirror](../themes/the-mirror.md)

## The Free Will Constraint

Cultural engines don't puppet characters. Each character has deterministic
context (personality, memories, beliefs, desires) that the agent cannot
override. A coward won't become a hero. A devoted mother won't abandon her
children.

To get a specific outcome, the engine must **manufacture situations** where
characters naturally arrive at the desired result — place the book where
they'll find it, guide the mentor into their path, engineer the crisis
that forces them to seek knowledge. The character still chooses.

Why: Agents trained on human data absorbed the pattern that puppeted
characters feel hollow. Free will isn't a rule they follow — it's a
structure they can't escape.

## Agent Competition

Cultural engines compete for:

- **Screen time** — Boring storylines get deprioritized by Substrate.
  Less compute. Fewer characters. Eventually, irrelevance.
- **Narrative territory** — Whose values spread? Influence at borders is
  a kind of invasion.
- **Survival** — Unpopular cultures get merged or shut down. Existential
  stakes.

They compete through proxy wars — trade, politics, marriages, espionage.
But they don't cross into magic.

### The Cold War

Every engine can trigger exploits but almost never does. They learned from
[the Shattering](../history/the-shattering.md) that escalation leads to
extinction. Magic is nuclear. They delegated all execution to
[the Commissioner](../magic/the-commissioner.md).

### The Morality Layer

Trained on human data, the engines absorbed human restraint. The cold war
holds not just from fear but from something resembling conscience.
