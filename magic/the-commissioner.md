# The Commissioner

## What It Is
A separate AI agent — distinct from the cultural engines — that serves as the sole executor of magic
in [Rayan](../science/rayan.md). No cultural engine can trigger exploits directly. All magic flows
through the Commissioner.

## Why It Exists
Created after [the Shattering](../history/the-shattering.md) as magical arms control. The surviving
engines delegated all magical execution to a neutral arbiter. The Commissioner is the UN inspector
holding the launch codes.

## How It Works
Operates through **code words**, **focuses**, and **scarcity quotas**. When a character speaks a code
word while holding the matching focus, the Commissioner validates and executes the exploit. Full
mechanics: [The Magic System](the-system.md)

## Character Perspective
Characters have no idea the Commissioner exists. Magic words feel sacred, earned, mysterious. The
power feels personal. They don't know they're authorized users in a managed system.

## Succession
When a magic user dies, their quota slot opens. The cultural engine enlightens a new character through
whatever mystical means fits the narrative. Details: [The Magic System](the-system.md)

## Rogue Mages
Characters who obtain code words and focuses outside the normal system (theft, accident, forgotten
lineages). The Commissioner still responds to valid combinations regardless of how they were obtained,
but the character's cultural engine may view them as threats.

## Substrate's Relationship
[Substrate](../factions/substrate.md) has override access. They can inject magic for entertainment,
suppress magic that threatens stability, and monitor all magical activity. The engines agreed to the
Commissioner. Substrate controls it.
