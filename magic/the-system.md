# The Magic System

## Core Truth
Magic is real — it exists in the code. What characters experience as supernatural power is
exploitation of the simulation's architecture. They don't know they're hacking their own world.

## Two Perspectives
- **Inside Rayan:** Magic is mysterious, dangerous, earned. Practitioners spend years learning to
  channel forces they don't understand. None know they're executing exploits.
- **Outside Rayan:** Magic is the gap between intended and actual simulation behavior. To Substrate
  engineers, a spell is a reproducible bug. To Elia, it may have been a gift — ways for Raya to have
  power she never had in real life.

## How It Works

### The Commissioner
Cultural engines don't execute magic themselves. All magic goes through
[the Commissioner](the-commissioner.md) — a separate, neutral agent. Only entity that can trigger
exploits. Origin: [The Shattering](../history/the-shattering.md)

### Focuses (Authentication Tokens)
Magic requires a physical anchor — a specially designated object bound to specific code words. The
Commissioner checks for a valid focus before executing. No focus, no magic.

**In-world:** wands, staves, enchanted rings, sacred relics, ritual daggers, ancient tomes
**In-system:** authentication tokens that limit agent power, create scarcity, and provide narrative
texture

### Code Words (API Calls)
Phrases paired to specific focuses. Character speaks a code word while holding the matching focus →
Commissioner executes the exploit. Two-factor authentication: focus (what you have) + code word
(what you know).

Cultural engines choose who learns the code words but can't implant knowledge directly — must
manufacture situations where characters naturally discover them. See:
[Free Will Constraint](../science/ai-architecture.md)

### Scarcity Quota
Each culture can only share code words with a limited number of characters at any time. Keeps magic
rare, prevents arms races, maintains the cold war.

## Who Can Use Magic

### The Enlightened
Characters who possess both a focus and the code words. They are chosen (engine selected), rare
(quota-limited), equipped (valid focus), and knowledgeable (know the paired words). They believe
they earned it through study, bloodline, sacrifice, or divine favor.

### How Characters Are Chosen
- **Bloodlines** — engine keeps giving slots to certain lineages
- **Study** — engine rewards persistent characters
- **Trauma** — emotional overflow triggers selection
- **Bargains** — narrative structures justifying selection
- **Random gift** — engine needed a mage and picked someone

### Death and Succession
When a mage dies, quota slot opens and focus becomes available. Creates: master-apprentice
traditions, magical dynasties, quests for power, murder for magic.

### Rogue Mages
Characters who acquire focus + code words without their engine's approval. Commissioner still
responds; the engine may view them as threats.

## Why Magic Is Rare
- **The Treaty** — post-Shattering constraints. Details: [The Shattering](../history/the-shattering.md)
- **Banned exploits** — spatial boundary manipulation, mass state corruption, physics engine
  overrides, recursive self-modification. Commissioner won't execute them. No focuses exist.
  Violation risks penalties up to cultural extinction.
- **Morality layer** — engines absorbed human ethics and restraint from training data
- **The quota** — each culture has only a handful of enlightened characters

### When Magic Appears
1. Enlightened character acts with focus in hand
2. Engine guides a mage to their focus when conventional solutions fail
3. Extreme emotion causes invocation while clutching focus
4. Substrate injects magic for entertainment (override access)
5. Elia's backdoors — triggers built to protect Raya, outside the treaty
6. Rogue mages operating outside the quota

## Implications
- **For characters:** Mages are respected, feared, or hunted. They have no idea an AI decided they
  needed magic.
- **For Substrate:** Magic generates great content but also risks. They monitor and sometimes
  suppress it.
- **For the story:** The deeper someone goes into magic, the closer they get to discovering the
  simulation's true nature.

## Connection to Raya

don't — could protect her, make her a target, or lead her toward the truth.
