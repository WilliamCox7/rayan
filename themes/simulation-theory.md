# Simulation Theory

## Core Idea
In [Rayan](../science/rayan.md), "is reality real?" isn't philosophy — it's literal truth. The world
is a simulation. The inhabitants don't know. But some might sense something is off.

## How It Manifests
- **Patterns** — characters too thematically coherent, conversations that feel scripted, coincidences
  too perfect, deja vu
- **Edges** — places no one has been (do they exist?), memories that don't add up, history that feels
  written rather than lived
- **The watchers** — characters who feel observed but can't explain why, a sense their lives have
  unseen stakes

## Characters and Suspicion
Most prone to questioning: philosophers, scholars, the neurotically observant, those who've
experienced glitches, anyone who gets close to [Raya](../characters/raya.md) and notices she's
different.

## The Question
If you discovered your world was a simulation, would you: break free? Despair? Decide it doesn't
matter? Try to find the creator?

## Connection to Elia
[Elia](../characters/elia.md) built this world to give his daughter a life — but also populated it
with people who believe they're real. If they discovered the truth, what would they think of their
god: a grieving man in San Francisco, watching them on a screen?
