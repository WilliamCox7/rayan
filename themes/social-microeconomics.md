# Social Microeconomics

## Core Idea
The story explores how people navigate systems of exchange—not just money, but status, favors, loyalty,
love. Everything has a cost. Everyone is calculating, consciously or not.

This isn't cynical. It's honest. People make trade-offs. They sacrifice. They invest in each other. The
economy of relationships is as real as the economy of coin.

## Dimensions

### Class & Resource Scarcity
- Who has wealth, land, magic, opportunity—and who doesn't
- The trade-offs people make to survive or climb
- How scarcity shapes behavior, morality, relationships

### Social Capital & Status
- Reputation as currency
- Who knows who, who owes who
- The invisible ledger of favors and debts
- Climbing and falling in society—what it costs, what it reveals

### Relationships as Transactions
- What people give and take from each other
- Love, loyalty, betrayal—all with stakes
- The uncomfortable question: is anything freely given?
- Romantic relationships complicated by power, need, ambition

## The Tangle
These aren't separate systems—they're woven together. A poor character with high social capital navigates
differently than a rich outcast. A romance between classes isn't just love; it's negotiation. Status
affects what resources you can access; resources affect what relationships you can afford.

## Tone
- **Romantic:** Love exists, but it's complicated. Desire and need get tangled.
- **Confusing:** Motivations are layered. People don't always know what they want.
- **Controversial:** The story doesn't moralize. Characters make ugly choices and sympathetic ones.
- **Nostalgic:** A world that feels lived-in, with history and weight. Loss lingers.

## Connection to the Meta Layer
Everything in [Rayan](../science/rayan.md) was built or emerged from [Elia's](../characters/elia.md)
design. The social systems, the economies, the relationships—are they his attempt to understand human
connection? To give [Raya](../characters/raya.md) a world rich enough to feel real? Or did these dynamics
emerge on their own once the simulation began running?

The audience watching the stream sees these dramas unfold. They have favorites. They argue about
characters' choices. The microeconomics of Rayan become entertainment for the real world.
