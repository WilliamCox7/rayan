# Social Microeconomics

## Core Idea
People navigate systems of exchange — not just money, but status, favors, loyalty, love. Everything
has a cost. Everyone is calculating, consciously or not. This isn't cynical; it's honest.

## Dimensions
- **Class & scarcity** — who has wealth, land, magic, opportunity. The trade-offs to survive or climb.
- **Social capital** — reputation as currency, invisible ledger of favors and debts, climbing and
  falling in society
- **Relationships as transactions** — what people give and take. Love, loyalty, betrayal with stakes.
  The uncomfortable question: is anything freely given?

## The Tangle
These systems are woven together. A poor character with high social capital navigates differently than
a rich outcast. Romance between classes isn't just love; it's negotiation.

## Tone
Romantic, confusing, controversial, nostalgic. Love exists but it's complicated. Motivations are
layered. No moralizing. Loss lingers.

## Meta Layer
Everything in [Rayan](../science/rayan.md) was built or emerged from
[Elia's](../characters/elia.md) design. Are the social systems his attempt to understand human
connection? To give [Raya](../characters/raya.md) a world rich enough to feel real? Or did they
emerge on their own? The audience watching the stream argues about characters' choices — Rayan's
microeconomics become real-world entertainment.
