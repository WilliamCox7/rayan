# The Edit

## Core Idea

Not everyone sees the same Rayan. The world is real — but truth is
curated.

[Substrate](../factions/substrate.md) offers two access modes:

1. **Produced Episodes** — Edited, narrative-driven, aired on schedule.
   Heroes and villains crafted by producers.
2. **Live Streams** — Raw, unedited feeds of any character. No framing.
   Just life.

Episode watchers accept the edit as truth. Live streamers see what's
really happening — and know the difference.

## The Divide

**Episode watchers** (majority): Consume as entertainment. Trust the
narrative. Have favorites based on portrayal. React to manufactured drama.

**Live streamers** (smaller community): Watch raw feeds for hours. Notice
when episodes contradict reality. Clash with episode watchers over "who a
character really is."

## How Substrate Edits

- **Selective framing** — Cruelty becomes "complexity." Kindness cut for
  pacing.
- **Narrative arcs** — Real events restructured to fit story beats
- **Villain creation** — Unflattering edits for unpopular characters
- **Romance manufacturing** — Barely-interacting characters edited into
  love stories
- **Context removal** — Justified actions look monstrous without the
  preceding scene

## Political Influence

Substrate shapes how audiences think about power, class, conflict — not
through propaganda but through story. Through who the heroes are. Through
which conflicts get resolved and how.

## The Meta Layer

Mirrors real-world media. Edited news, algorithms choosing what you see,
history written by whoever controls the story. When live streamers argue
with episode watchers about "the truth," they're reenacting debates
happening in the real world.

## Big Brother Element

Substrate sees everything and chooses what to show. The audience is
surveilled through viewing habits. The watchers think they're observing.
They're also being observed.
