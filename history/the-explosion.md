# The Explosion

## Overview
- **Time Period:** TBD (recent—within the last several years before the story's present)
- **Location:** Beirut, Lebanon
- **Type:** Industrial disaster / government negligence

## What Happened
A massive explosion tore through the city, killing hundreds and devastating entire neighborhoods. The
cause: hazardous materials that had been improperly stored for years in a populated area. Officials knew
about the danger. They did nothing.

It wasn't war. It wasn't terrorism. It was the government's indifference to the lives of its own people.

## The Betrayal
This is what makes the explosion different from an attack by an external enemy. The people who were
supposed to protect the city—the officials, the institutions, the system itself—allowed this to happen
through corruption, incompetence, and neglect.

For Elia, this reframed everything. Raya wasn't killed by an enemy he could hate or a tragedy he could
accept. She was killed by the slow rot of a country that had stopped caring about its people. There was
no one to fight. There was only abandonment.

## Aftermath
- No accountability. Investigations stalled, blocked by the same people responsible.
- Mass emigration. Skilled professionals fled, seeing no future in a country that would let its children
  die through neglect.
- Elia left Lebanon and never returned. He couldn't forgive. He couldn't forget.

## Casualties
- [Raya](../characters/raya.md) — Killed in the blast, shortly before she was to accomplish something her
  father was eagerly awaiting

## Legacy
For Elia, the explosion ended one world and began another. In Rayan, there are no corrupt officials. No
slow decay. No senseless, preventable catastrophes. It is a world built in opposition to everything that
killed his daughter.
