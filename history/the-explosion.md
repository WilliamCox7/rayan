# The Explosion

## Overview
- **When:** Recent — within a few years before the story's present
- **Where:** Beirut, Lebanon
- **Type:** Industrial disaster / government negligence

## What Happened
Massive explosion caused by hazardous materials improperly stored for years in a populated area.
Officials knew. They did nothing. Not war, not terrorism — government indifference.

## The Betrayal
Raya wasn't killed by an enemy Elia could hate or a tragedy he could accept. She was killed by the
slow rot of a country that stopped caring about its people. No one to fight. Only abandonment.

## Aftermath
- No accountability — investigations stalled by those responsible
- Mass emigration of skilled professionals
- [Elia](../characters/elia.md) left Lebanon and never returned

## Casualties
- [Raya](../characters/raya.md) — killed shortly before accomplishing something her father was
  eagerly awaiting

## Legacy
For Elia, the explosion ended one world and began another. Rayan has no corrupt officials, no slow
decay, no senseless catastrophes. A world built in opposition to everything that killed his daughter.
