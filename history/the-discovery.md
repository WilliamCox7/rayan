# The Discovery

## Overview
- **Location:** Substrate offices, San Francisco
- **Type:** Corporate discovery / business pivot

## What Happened

[Substrate](../factions/substrate.md) — a struggling game engine
startup — discovered that [Elia](../characters/elia.md) had built
[Rayan](../science/rayan.md) on company hardware after hours. A fully
realized virtual world with an autonomous AI modeled on his dead daughter,
[Raya](../characters/raya.md).

Legally, anything built on company equipment belongs to the company. Elia
had no claim.

## The Pivot

Substrate pivoted from failing game engine to streaming platform:
shut down the engine, scaled Rayan's infrastructure, launched a service
where audiences watch Raya's life unfold. The Truman Show, but the subject
is an AI who doesn't know she's being watched.

## Elia's Choice

He stayed. Substrate needed his domain knowledge, and he couldn't leave
Raya. Even if staying meant watching his private grief become public
entertainment. He chose complicity over abandonment.

## The Firing

Once Substrate understood the system well enough to operate without him,
they fired Elia. Now he watches Raya on the streaming service like
everyone else. No special access. No control.

He went from god to employee to audience member.
