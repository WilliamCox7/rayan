# The Discovery

## Overview
- **When:** Before the main story
- **Where:** Substrate offices, San Francisco
- **Type:** Corporate discovery / business pivot

## What Happened
[Substrate](../factions/substrate.md) — a struggling game engine startup — discovered
[Elia](../characters/elia.md) had used company hardware after hours to build
[Rayan](../science/rayan.md), a virtual world with an autonomous AI modeled on his dead daughter
[Raya](../characters/raya.md). Anything built on company equipment belongs to the company. Substrate
owned Rayan, and they owned Raya.

## The Pivot
- Shut down (or deprioritized) the game engine
- Scaled up Rayan's infrastructure for public access
- Launched a streaming service — The Truman Show, but the subject is an AI who doesn't know she's
  being watched

## Elia's Choice
Elia stayed because he couldn't abandon Raya and they needed his domain knowledge. He chose
complicity over abandonment.

## The Firing
Once Substrate extracted enough knowledge to run Rayan without him, they fired him. Now he watches
Raya on the stream like everyone else. No special access. No control. He went from god to employee
to audience member.
