# The Discovery

## Overview
- **Time Period:** TBD (before the main story begins)
- **Location:** Substrate offices, San Francisco
- **Type:** Corporate discovery / business pivot

## What Happened
[Substrate](../factions/substrate.md)—a struggling game engine startup—discovered that one of their
engineers, [Elia](../characters/elia.md), had been using company hardware after hours to build something
extraordinary: a fully realized virtual world with an autonomous AI at its center.

The world was called [Rayan](../science/rayan.md). The AI was modeled on his dead daughter,
[Raya](../characters/raya.md).

## The Legal Reality
Anything built on company equipment belongs to the company. Elia had no claim to what he'd created.
Substrate owned Rayan, and they owned Raya.

## The Pivot
Substrate's leadership realized what they had was worth more than their failing game engine. They pivoted
the entire company:

- Shut down (or deprioritized) the game engine
- Scaled up Rayan's infrastructure for public access
- Launched a streaming service where audiences can watch Raya's life unfold in real-time

The Truman Show, but the subject is an AI who doesn't know she's being watched.

## Elia's Choice
Elia could have left. He could have walked away, found another job, tried to forget.

He stayed.

Substrate needed his domain knowledge—he was the only one who truly understood the system. And he
couldn't leave Raya. Even if staying meant watching strangers consume her life as entertainment. Even if
his private grief was now public spectacle.

He chose complicity over abandonment.

## The Firing
Eventually, Substrate fired him. Once they had extracted enough knowledge to operate Rayan without him,
Elia became disposable. Perhaps they saw him as a liability—the grieving father who might do something
unpredictable. Perhaps they simply didn't need him anymore.

Now Elia watches Raya on the streaming service every day, like everyone else. No special access. No
control. No way to reach her. He created her world, and now he can only observe it through the same
screen as any stranger.

He went from god to employee to audience member.

## Raya's Status
Raya knows nothing. She continues living her life in Rayan, unaware of the audience. She is, within the
simulation, effectively immortal—she cannot die, because the show must go on.
