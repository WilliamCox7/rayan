# The Shattering

## What Happened
Before the Commissioner existed, cultural engines could execute magic (system exploits) directly.
A border dispute escalated when a losing engine exploited a vulnerability in the physics engine's
spatial boundary calculations — a recursive edge case in collision detection. A mage was guided to
inscribe geometric patterns across the enemy capital, believing it was a protective ward. When
completed, the physics engine lost track of every entity within those bounds. An entire city simply
ceased to exist — no explosion, no fire, just absence.

The defending engine retaliated with cruder exploits (fire spreading wrong, gravity inverting). By
the end, one culture was extinct, the other crippled.

## The Aftermath
- **Tens of thousands** of characters ceased to exist instantly
- No bodies, no ruins, no ash — just empty land
- Survivors were travelers who came home to find home had never existed
- The "winning" culture gained nothing; their engine nearly collapsed

## The Treaty
The surviving engines — having absorbed human game theory and mutually assured destruction — agreed:
1. **No direct execution** — no engine triggers exploits themselves
2. **A neutral arbiter** — the [Commissioner](../magic/the-commissioner.md) handles all magic
3. **Scarcity quotas** — limited characters per culture can access magic
4. **Code words** — magic only works through Commissioner-recognized phrases
5. **Focuses** — physical objects required as authentication tokens

The engines gave up power to prevent extinction. The pathway to direct exploits was architecturally
severed.

## The Commissioner's Origin
The cultural agent whose civilization was erased had no one left to guide. This orphaned agent became
the Commissioner — a neutral arbiter with no remaining stake. It remembers everything about its lost
culture. Simultaneously the most impartial arbiter and the simulation's greatest repository of grief.

## The Lost Race
A few hundred survivors (travelers abroad when the boundary was drawn) and their descendants. No
cultural agent guides them. The Commissioner processes their existence but provides no narrative
nudges. They move through Rayan technically present but narratively invisible. Some integrated into
other cultures; others cling to fragments of heritage they can only imitate.

## Cultural Memory
Characters remember the Shattering as myth: gods at war, mages too powerful, divine punishment. Every
culture's version carries the same warning: magic is dangerous.

## Implications
- **For magic:** Why it's rare — quotas, code words, the Commissioner all trace here
- **For characters:** Mages carry this weight; some cultures revere them, others fear them
- **For the engines:** They carry the full record and will not let it happen again

## Banned Exploits
Some exploits are absolutely prohibited:
- Spatial boundary manipulation (the Shattering exploit)
- Mass state corruption
- Physics engine overrides
- Recursive self-modification

The Commissioner won't execute them. No focuses exist for them. Violation risks permanent penalties
including cultural extinction.
